Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

This policy covers Klik and Klik Studio for macOS (together, “the apps”). The short version: your screenshots and recordings stay on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on a feature that does so, and then it goes only where you choose.

What stays on your Mac

  • Screenshots, recordings, edits, captions and settings are stored on your Mac.
  • Captions are made with on-device speech recognition. Your audio is not sent anywhere.
  • The apps contain no analytics, advertising or tracking, and we run no servers that receive your data.

Google Drive (Klik Studio)

Klik Studio can back up your recordings to your own Google Drive. This is off until you choose Connect in Settings › Storage and sign in with Google.

  • What we ask for: permission to create and manage files that Klik Studio itself adds to your Drive (the drive.file scope), and your email address, which is shown in Settings so you can see which account is connected. Klik Studio cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.
  • How it is used: Klik Studio uploads the recordings you choose (or, if you turn on automatic upload, recordings you save) into a “Klik Studio” folder in your Drive, and reads those files back only to keep the backup up to date and to give you a link to them.
  • Where it goes: directly from your Mac to Google. The developer never receives your files, your email address or your Google sign-in.
  • Storage: the sign-in token is kept in your Mac’s keychain. Uploaded recordings stay in your Drive until you delete them there.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Disconnecting: choose Disconnect in Settings › Storage. This revokes Klik Studio’s access and deletes the token from your Mac. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Klik Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other optional features

Some features send data only when you set them up, and only to the service you configure: uploading a screenshot to your own storage or server, sending notes to Notion, syncing tasks to a server you choose, recognising text with Google Cloud Vision using your own key, or importing a video into Klik Studio from a YouTube or Vimeo link, which asks that site for the video’s details. Data sent to those services is governed by their privacy policies.

Updates

To check for updates and download add-ons, the apps fetch files from GitHub. GitHub may log your IP address as part of serving those files.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
